Interval-ending timestamps use the source NEM clock (AEST, UTC+10). For daily grouping, subtract five minutes before assigning a date. Negative prices are retained. Daily means use 288 observations. Spread is always TAS1 minus VIC1.

These descriptive results do not establish causes. Generation mix, network constraints, flows, weather and bidding require additional analysis. Historical wholesale prices do not predict future prices or household bills.
